What is Loanly? ⌄

Loanly is a digital lending management platform for tracking borrowers, repayments, balances, and lending performance.

Who can use Loanly? ⌄

Loanly is built for small business lenders, financing groups, cooperatives, and individual lenders.

Is Loanly secure? ⌄

Yes. All records are encrypted and accessible only through your secured account.

Does Loanly process payments? ⌄

Not yet. Loanly currently focuses on loan tracking, reminders, and analytics.

How do I start? ⌄

Create an account, add your borrowers, create loans, and start managing repayments instantly.
